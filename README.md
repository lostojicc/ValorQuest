ValorQuest is a mobile RPG productivity app that transforms your everyday responsibilities into an immersive adventure. Instead of traditional to-do lists, users create custom quests based on real-life tasks—like studying, exercising, or doing chores.

Progress in real life directly impacts your in-game character. Complete quests to gain experience (XP), earn currency, unlock gear, and grow stronger as you advance through your journey.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🎯 **Custom Quests**  
  Create tasks with difficulty levels and rewards based on real-life goals.

- ⚔️ **Character Progression**  
  Gain XP, level up, and develop your character over time.

- 💰 **In-Game Economy**  
  Earn currency and purchase weapons, armor, and upgrades.

- 🧙 **Classes & Playstyles**  
  Choose a class and specialize your progression path.

- 👹 **Boss Battles**  
  Face bosses tied to major real-life milestones and achievements.

- 📈 **Progress Tracking**  
  Monitor streaks, consistency, and personal growth.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Android (Java)
- SQLite / Room Database
- Firebase (authentication, analytics)
